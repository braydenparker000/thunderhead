# The daily pictures of Ink and Lamp (frontend/look/pictures/)

Every picture here is a reproduction of a work in the public domain, taken from the source named,
under the licence that source states. Engravings (`ink/`) are shown as dark ink on the grey page;
paintings (`lamp/`) in colour on black. Each is cropped to the header's shape; the `ink/` ones are
also turned to grey and printed on the page's ground. Verses are from the King James Bible (Pure
Cambridge Edition) and the Book of Mormon (Project Gutenberg #17), checked word for word.
A file's name carries the first 8 hex of the SHA-256 of its bytes, so a picture never changes
under one name (`tests/look.test.mjs`).
