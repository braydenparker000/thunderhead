# Your card

One page for the months you use only the site on your phone. Keep it somewhere you can find it without the
phone.

## What the site tells you, and where

- **The Briefing** (swipe right from home) says when something needs you, one line each:
  - "Today's database use is at 62% of the free plan's daily limit." From 60 %. Past 80 % it adds when backups
    and uploads start again. Nothing is lost; heavy work waits for the next day.
  - "Muse hasn't checked in since yesterday." Music uploads wait until Muse is back.
  - A line that starts "From yesterday" or "Couldn't refresh just now": the page is an older copy. Pull down to
    try again.
- **A push** when the day's database use is high, for reminders you set, and when someone signs in on a new
  device or asks to reset your password. Pushes wait until your quiet hours end.
- **Home** shows a red card for anything about your sign-in. Read it before you tap.

## Repair and Copy report

Both are in **Settings › About**.

- **Repair** sets the site on this phone right. Try it first when a page looks wrong or will not open.
- **Copy report** copies a report of how the site is doing on this phone. Bring it when you ask for help.

## If the phone signs you out

1. **Your password.** Sign in as usual.
2. **Your recovery code** (25 characters, written down when you set up). On the sign-in page tap
   "Forgot password?", then "I have my recovery code". It works once; the site shows you a new one at once.
   Write the new one down.
3. **Neither one.** The reset ticket: on the same page, "I've lost it too". It needs GitHub (one setting and one
   button there) and then waits **24 hours** before it works. Any phone still signed in can cancel it, so a
   stranger cannot use it while you are signed in somewhere. This is the one way back that needs help from
   outside the phone.

## What keeps working when the mind is off

The site, Music, Podcasts, Guitar, Study, Photos and Astra. The Briefing's weather, Due, Today, Holidays and the
day's line. Reminders you set ("remind me at 5 to call the bank") and their pushes. Going quiet. The weekly
backup and its monthly check. Your messages are kept; the mind answers them when it is back, and home says so.

## What needs Muse

Adding songs to Music. A message that starts "@muse" asks it, and its progress shows in today's conversation.
Everything else works without it.

## What is on this phone only

Lost with the phone, or when its site data is cleared: songs added from files on this phone and Music's own
library and playlists, the DJ's learning, Podcasts downloads, My Media's settings, the data use counts in
Settings, and a reset code made on this phone. Everything else is kept on your account and comes back when you
sign in on a new phone.

## When to come back for help

- The same red line or failed page for two days, after Repair.
- You cannot sign in with your password or your recovery code.
- A push or card about a sign-in or a reset that was not you.
- The Briefing says the database use is high three days running.

Bring one **Copy report** from Settings › About, and say what you tapped and what you saw.
