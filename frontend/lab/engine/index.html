Engine test

The engine's test bench: your RAF opened in the browser (rawler), binned to the 2576 px editing copy, and developed on the phone's graphics chip through your profiles. It shows what each step costs on this phone; the switches turn one step off, to compare.

Test this phone

One tap checks that this phone's graphics chip develops a known, made-up photo correctly, then times every stage on the newest photo in your Library: decoding, the first picture, a slider, a screenful at 100 %, a full-size export, and the memory it took. Nothing leaves the phone: copy the result and paste it where it is wanted.








The network on this phone


The neural network that makes the picture at 100 % and in an export, measured here: how long one window of a photo takes on this phone's graphics chip, where in its eleven passes the time goes, whether the passes written other ways are faster on this chip and make as true a picture, how fast the phone's processor cores would do the same work, and how much the two get through working at once. It takes a few minutes and warms the phone. The ways it finds faster and as true are kept for the editor. Its numbers go with the editor's timings to your own storage when that setting is on; nothing else leaves the phone.
